# Reverse-Engineering Notes

## Observed page structure

1. Full-viewport hero
2. Floating circular or pill-shaped menu
3. Central circular invitation composition
4. Couple story
5. Vertical schedule
6. Venue and map
7. RSVP callout
8. Accordion-style FAQ
9. Minimal footer

## Visual system

- Soft ivory and pale sage palette
- Script typography for names and romantic copy
- Serif typography for event headings and body emphasis
- Small sans-serif utility labels
- Organic circular shapes and botanical accents
- High whitespace and centered hierarchy
- Warm, editorial, paper-like surfaces

## Responsive behavior

- One-column layout below 700px
- Menu becomes a smaller floating control
- Schedule time column remains compact on mobile
- The RSVP button links to an external form
- Native `<details>` provides accessible FAQ behavior
- Reduced-motion preference disables smooth behavior
